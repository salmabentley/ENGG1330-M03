**The Corporate Defense:** Vance Construction CEO Thomas Vance formally deflected liability, presenting subcontractor procurement logs claiming that all defective materials were outsourced to an external supplier: **Gabrielle [REDACTED]**, sole proprietor of an independent material fabrication entity.
