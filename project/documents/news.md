# ||THE METROPOLITAN GAZETTE||
> JULY 14, 2024

## VANCE CONSTRUCTION CLEARED IN FATAL SITE COLLAPSE

**Widow Vows Justice After Involuntary Manslaughter Charges Dismissed**

**METROPOLIS** — All criminal charges against Vance Infrastructure & Construction Group were dismissed yesterday following an eight-month legal battle over the death of site worker Arthur Lan.
Lan, 42, died in November 2023 when a sub-level scaffolding structure collapsed at the company’s Sector 7 site. While initial safety audits cited compromised steel bracing and rushed construction timelines, Judge Ronald Sterling ruled there was "insufficient admissible evidence" to hold corporate management criminally liable.

**Wealth Over Safety**

The trial was defined by a stark resource imbalance. Armed with top corporate defense attorneys, Vance Infrastructure successfully challenged key prosecution reports, while critical site inspection logs went missing prior to evidence submission. The court ultimately classified the collapse as an "unforeseen equipment anomaly."

Outside the courtroom, Arthur’s widow, Eleanor Lan, delivered a sharp response to the verdict:
> "My husband warned his supervisors three times about those supports. He died because speed was prioritized over human life. Vance Construction didn't win because they were innocent—they won because they had the money to buy silence and erase their paper trail. But justice has a way of catching up."
With all charges dropped, Vance Infrastructure is set to move forward with its multi-million-dollar city contracts, leaving the Lan family without legal recourse.
