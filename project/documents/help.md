# Game Rules
## 1. Story Background
You are a detective who has been invited to an exclusive mansion party by your former client, Vance Thomas. What begins as an ordinary social gathering quickly turns into a murder investigation when Vance Thomas is found dead in his office. 

With four potential suspects and limited time and resources, you must carefully examine evidence, interview witnesses, and uncover hidden motives. Every decision matters. The truth is waiting to be discovered, but only a skilled detective can solve the case. 

## 2. Objective
Your job is to identify who, among the four suspects, is responsible for the murder of Vance Thomas.  
To succeed, you need to:
* Gather evidence from the crime scene as much as possible to infer the murder motive and narrow down your suspicious individuals
* Analyze and draw connections between the clues found in documents and personal belongings
* Critically and carefully question the suspects
* Make a final accusation
You win if your accusation is correct and lose otherwise.

## 3. Answer Inputs
You should always enter your responses using the exact format requested by the game. Unrecognized input may result in a penalty. 
Movement Controls
In Room 2, use the following keys to move around the map:

Key | Action                 |
-----------------------------|
W   | Move forward one unit  |
A   | Move left one unit     |
S   | Move backward one unit |
D   | Move right one unit    |

## 4 Time and Energy System
Time spent investigating Room 1 directly affects the amount of energy available in Room 2. The longer you remain in Room 1, the less energy you will have for interviewing suspects. 
Energy Formula

### Energy = ((600 - Time Spent in seconds ) / 600 ) x 100
Example:
If you leave Room 1 after 2 minutes, your energy level will be: 
Energy = ((10-2)/10) * 100
Energy = 80%

Important Notes:
* You may leave Room 1 at any time once you visit all 3 parts: dead body, computer, and safe
* Once you enter Room 2, you cannot return to Room 1
* Room 1 contains critical evidence necessary for solving the case
* You should balance investigation time and available energy carefully

## 5. Transition to Room 2
When you decide to leave Room 1:
* You already visited all the parts in Room 1
* Your remaining time is converted into energy
* Room 1 becomes permanently inaccessible
* You are transferred to Room 2
* The suspect investigation phase begins
This decision cannot be reversed.

## 6. Suspect Investigation Phase: Room 2
### Overview
Room 2 contains a map showing the locations of all four suspects.
You control your detective character and move across the map using the movement controls
Each movement consumes energy
The purpose of Room 2 is to gather additional information and compare suspect statements against the evidence collected in Room 1.

### Approaching Suspects
Once your character reaches a suspect’s location:
* A conversation begins
* Dialogue would appear
* You may choose questions to ask
* The suspect responds
* New information may be revealed

Important Notes:
* Suspects are designed to have different characteristics. Disrespecting hot-tempered characters may result in energy deduction
* Enter your responses using the exact format requested by the game. Unrecognized input may result in energy penalty
* Carefully evaluate every statement. Not all information is equally useful

## 7. Final Accusation
At any point during Room 2, you may decide that you have gathered enough evidence to solve the case.
To make a final accusation:
* Select the accusation option by pressing P in room 2
* Choose one of the four suspects
* Confirm your decision
* Once confirmed, the accusation cannot be changed. The game immediately proceeds to the result screen.

Important Notes:
In case your energy reaches zero, you are immediately required to make the final accusation

## 8. Interconnected Challenges and Tips 
These are the main interconnected challenges of the game:
* Clue in one object may affect your ability to unlock and visit another part of the room
* Information you gather in Room 1 will affect your knowledge about each suspect in Room 2
* You don’t need to investigate all the documents and challenges. However, you have to visit all parts in Room 1 before moving to Room 2
* Your time spent in Room 1 will affect your energy level in Room 2. You need energy to move around the mansion and to investigate the suspects in Room 2

With these interconnected challenges, your chances of solving the case improve when you:
* Thoroughly investigate Room 1 before leaving
* Pay close attention to documents and personal records
* Compare suspect statements with physical evidence
* Look for inconsistencies in stories and timelines
* Manage your time wisely to preserve energy from Room 2
* Avoid making conclusions based on a single piece of evidence
